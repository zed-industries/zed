# `zero_width_bitmap_strike.ttf`

A 1KB font generated by `generate_zero_width_strike.py` (fontTools), used only by
tests. It reproduces the crash in [#62160](https://github.com/zed-industries/zed/issues/62160).

It carries EBLC/EBDT embedded bitmap strikes at four sizes, matching the shape
real monospace faces ship:

| ppem | bitDepth | indexFormat | imageFormat | `space` mask | `A` mask |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| 10   | 1        | 3           | 1           | 0x0          | 4x6      |
| 11   | 1        | 3           | 1           | 0x0          | 5x7      |
| 12   | 1        | 3           | 1           | 0x0          | 6x8      |
| 13   | 1        | 3           | 1           | 0x0          | 7x9      |

The zero-width `space` mask is the degenerate case. A 1-bit strike is decoded as
`BitmapFormat::Alpha(1)`, and swash then iterates the mask with
`src.chunks(((w * bits) + 7) / 8)`. With `w == 0` that chunk size is `0`, and
`slice::chunks(0)` panics.

The outlines are plain rectangles authored here rather than derived from any
existing typeface, so the file carries no third-party licence. Regenerate with
`python3 generate_zero_width_strike.py`. The generator pins `head.created` and
`head.modified`, so the output is byte-reproducible and can be diffed against
the committed file.
